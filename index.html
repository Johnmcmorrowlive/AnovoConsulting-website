Our Founder

John McMorrow

March 2026

John McMorrow is the founder of Anovo Consulting. Over the past 30 years at Microsoft Ireland, he has led and managed high‑performing teams across the cloud computing business. His expertise spans project and program management, customer engagement and customer success, and, most recently, leadership of solution architecture teams for the Power Platform with a strong focus on Generative AI.

Known for the tag-line "Get it done," John brings a pragmatic, outcome‑driven approach to problem solving and solution delivery.

He established Anovo Consulting to help organisations achieve their strategic objectives with clarity, execution excellence, and measurable impact. John looks forward to partnering with you to accelerate your success.

Biography

Known for the tag-line "Get it done," John brings a pragmatic, outcome‑driven approach to problem solving and solution delivery. He established Anovo Consulting to help organisations achieve their strategic objectives with clarity, execution excellence, and measurable impact. John looks forward to partnering with you to accelerate your success.

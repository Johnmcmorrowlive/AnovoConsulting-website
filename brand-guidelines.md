# ANOVO Consulting Brand Guidelines

## Introduction
ANOVO Consulting is committed to maintaining a strong and cohesive brand identity. This document outlines the brand standards that should be adhered to in all communications and representations of the brand.

## Logo Usage
- The ANOVO logo must not be altered or distorted in any way.
- Always maintain a clear space around the logo; this should be equal to the height of the logo itself.

## Color Palette
- Primary Colors:
  - ANOVO Blue: #003366
  - ANOVO Grey: #A6A6A6

- Secondary Colors:
  - Light Blue: #A3C1E0
  - Dark Grey: #666666

## Typography
- Primary Typeface: Helvetica Neue
  - Regular: 11pt
  - Bold: 11pt

## Imagery
- Use high-quality images that reflect professionalism and align with the brand values.

## Tone of Voice
- Communication should be professional, clear, and concise. Avoid jargon unless necessary for targeted audiences.

## Application Guidelines
- Ensure consistent application of these guidelines across all platforms and media, including digital and print formats.
